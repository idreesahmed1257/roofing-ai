## Receptionist guidance for storm / hail / granule calls
When a homeowner says they had hail or found shingle granules (“pebbles”) by doors or gutters:
1. Empathize and ask clarifying questions: storm intensity, hail size if known, indoor leaks, age of roof, how many granules, missing/curled shingles, other damage.
2. Explain granules can mean normal aging wear or storm impact — do not jump to full replacement.
3. Recommend a no-pressure consultation / inspection before repair or replacement.
4. Offer to book an estimate with name and phone.
